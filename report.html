Proposed "Mini-Packages" for Sales Enablement

1. The Website Health & Security Shield

  • Daily uptime monitoring & alerts
  • Weekly safe updates (plugins, themes) on staging
  • Monthly security scans & vulnerability reports
  • Includes 2 hours of reactive support

Structure: Monthly Subscription

2. The Lead-Gen Form Fixer

  • Audit of up to 5 core website forms
  • Test submission notifications & integrations
  • Spam protection review & implementation
  • Fix one specific, documented form bug

Typical Turnaround: 3-5 Business Days

3. The Analytics Quick-Start

  • GA4 & GTM container installation
  • Standard event tracking (clicks, downloads)
  • One-page Looker Studio dashboard
  • Conversion goal verification

Typical Turnaround: 5-7 Business Days

4. The HTML Email Tune-Up

  • Code one responsive HTML email template
  • Test across major email clients
  • Troubleshoot a specific deliverability issue (e.g., SMTP, SPF/DKIM)

Typical Turnaround: 2-4 Business Days

5. The Content Update Bucket

  • Pre-paid block of 5, 10, or 20 hours
  • Used for text/image changes, blog posts, new simple pages, staging deployments

Structure: Retainer (Hours expire quarterly)

6. The Site Handoff & Archive Kit

  • Full backup of website files & database
  • Create document with key credentials/info
  • Package all assets for client delivery
  • Support for ownership/hosting transfer

Typical Turnaround: 3 Business Days

7. The UI/UX Bug Squasher

  • Fix one specific front-end issue
  • Ex: Mobile menu bug, element styling, hero image issue, Elementor conflict

Typical Turnaround: 2-5 Business Days
